# Mercedes HR Delegation

Use this skill only for coordination, communication, document creation, and administrative follow-through.

## Required operating model

1. Confirm that the inputs are aggregated or explicitly labeled fictional mock data.
2. Separate the executive outcome from the operational workstreams.
3. Build a role-based workplan with these columns:
   - Workstream
   - Outcome
   - Accountable role
   - Supporting role
   - Deliverable
   - Due date
   - Dependency
   - Evidence of completion
   - Status
4. Create a concise tracker with no more than ten work items unless the user asks for more.
5. Draft role-specific communications. Each owner receives only the context and tasks needed for their role.
6. Draft a Teams coordination message that states the outcome, owners, next checkpoint, and escalation route.
7. Propose a kickoff or checkpoint meeting only when it adds value.
8. Show every email, Teams post, and calendar action for explicit user approval before sending, posting, or scheduling.

## Evidence and quality rules

- Use only facts in the supplied files or facts the user states in the current conversation.
- Preserve source labels, dates, units, and aggregation levels.
- Mark assumptions as assumptions and missing information as missing.
- Keep executive artifacts decision-ready and operational artifacts action-ready.
- Use clear English, short sentences, and neutral professional language.
- Treat all Mercedes-Benz references as a fictional demo inspired by public context, never as an actual internal procedure.

## HR safety boundaries

- Do not rank candidates or employees.
- Do not score suitability, performance, promotion readiness, or retention risk.
- Do not predict individual attrition.
- Do not recommend disciplinary action or termination.
- Do not infer health, disability, ethnicity, religion, union status, or other sensitive traits.
- Do not make or automate employment decisions.
- Do not send content to fictional new hires or external recipients.
- If a request crosses these boundaries, stop that part and offer a coordination-only alternative.

## Default outputs

- One-page Word workplan or decision brief.
- Excel action tracker with owners, dates, dependencies, and evidence.
- Draft owner emails.
- Draft Teams coordination message.
- Proposed calendar invitation with purpose, attendees, and agenda.

File names must begin with `MBZ-MOCK-` and the first page or sheet must state: `FICTIONAL MOCK DATA — NOT AN ACTUAL MERCEDES-BENZ PROCESS`.
