## Demo Script

### HOOK (5 sec)
*"It's Monday 8am. 47 unread emails. Three meetings. Zero idea where to start. Watch what one Copilot prompt does."*

### SETUP (10 sec)
Open Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (BizChat) — the purple Copilot icon, not Teams chat.
The viewer sees a clean, empty chat interface. No files open. No attachments.
*"This is BizChat — it has access to your entire Microsoft 365 graph. Emails, calendar, Teams messages, documents."*

### PROMPT (10 sec)
Type (or paste) the prompt:

```
Give me a Monday morning briefing:

1. Summarize my unread emails from the last 48 hours, grouped by priority 
   (Urgent / FYI / Can Wait). For Urgent items, include the sender and 
   one-line action needed.
2. List today's meetings with start time, attendees, and a 2-sentence 
   prep note for each based on recent email context.
3. Flag any action items from last week that appear overdue — where I 
   committed to something but haven't replied or followed up.
4. Draft a quick Monday status update I can send to my team — 3 bullet 
   points max, covering what I'm focusing on today.
```

*"That's it. One prompt. No attachments. No file uploads. Let's go."*

### MAGIC (40 sec)
Watch Copilot work in real time:

- It scans the last 48 hours of emails from Microsoft Exchange
- Groups them into Urgent / FYI / Can Wait — with summaries, not full threads
- Pulls today's calendar entries and cross-references email threads with those attendees
- Identifies threads where you said "I'll get back to you" or "Let me check" — and haven't
- Drafts a 3-bullet status update you can send to your team in one click

**Highlight moments to point out in the recording:**
- The "Urgent" category is short — usually 2-4 emails that actually matter
- Copilot connects a meeting at 10am with an email from that person last Thursday
- The overdue items section is where Copilot earns its keep — it found things you forgot

### RESULT (15 sec)
*"In 60 seconds: my inbox is triaged, my meetings are prepped, my commitments are surfaced, and I have a draft status update ready to send. I haven't opened Outlook once."*

**Show the output structure:**
- 📌 Urgent (2 items with action + sender)
- 📋 FYI (5 items, brief summaries)
- ⏸️ Can Wait (rest)
- 📅 Today's meetings with prep notes
- ⚠️ Overdue follow-up: "Still waiting for your response on the Q2 budget approval — Sarah (Marketing), sent last Wednesday"
- ✉️ Team status update draft ready to copy

### CTA (10 sec)
*"This is Copilot Chat with Work IQ — it's not searching the web, it's reading YOUR Microsoft 365 data with your permission. Try it tomorrow morning before you open your inbox."*

*"Drop a 👋 if you start your Mondays this way now."*
