ABOUT THE ROLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You will own the product strategy for our third-party integrations platform —
the layer that connects our core SaaS product to 60+ partner ecosystems
(CRM, ERP, marketing automation, data warehouses). This is a high-visibility,
high-ownership role sitting at the intersection of product, engineering, and
business development.

You will work closely with the Engineering Lead (Integrations), the
Partnerships team, and enterprise customers to define what gets built,
validate that it delivers value, and communicate the roadmap to stakeholders.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Define and own the Platform Integrations roadmap for FY2026 and beyond
2. Conduct customer discovery interviews (min. 4/month in first quarter)
3. Write clear, actionable product specs (PRDs) in Confluence — no ambiguity
4. Prioritize backlog with engineering using RICE or similar framework
5. Run weekly sync with engineering pod (8 engineers); bi-weekly with partners
6. Define success metrics for every feature; monitor post-launch
7. Present roadmap and progress in quarterly Business Reviews
8. Collaborate with GTM team on launch briefs and sales enablement
9. Manage relationships with 5 strategic integration partners
10. Represent product in enterprise customer escalations
