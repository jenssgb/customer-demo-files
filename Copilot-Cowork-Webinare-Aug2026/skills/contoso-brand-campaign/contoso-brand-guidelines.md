# Contoso Brand Guidelines (companion for the Contoso Brand Campaign skill)

Apply this brand system to every email, blurb, and asset the skill produces.

## Brand colours

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Contoso Blue (Primary) | `#0F6CBD` | Headlines, primary CTA button, links |
| Deep Blue | `#0A4A82` | Header band, footer |
| Ink | `#1B1B1F` | Body text |
| Slate | `#5B5F66` | Secondary text, captions |
| Accent Teal | `#2AA198` | Small highlights, success states |
| Alert Amber | `#C8791A` | Deadlines, limited-time offers (use sparingly) |
| Cloud | `#F4F7FB` | Email background, cards |

## Typography

- Headlines: Segoe UI Semibold. Sentence case, never ALL CAPS.
- Body: Segoe UI / Arial / Helvetica, 15-16px, generous line spacing.
- One H1 per email (the headline), H2 only if the email has two clear sections.

## Voice

- Confident, helpful, human. Plain business English (or German for DACH).
- Short sentences. Lead with the reader's benefit, then the proof.
- No hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), no exclamation stacking.
- Inclusive, direct, respectful. Address the reader as "you".

## Logo & layout

- Contoso wordmark top-left of the header band, never distorted or recoloured.
- Deep Blue header band, Cloud body, generous whitespace.
- One primary CTA rendered as a Contoso Blue button-style line.

## Standard sign-off

```
Warm regards,
The Contoso Team

Contoso Ltd · One Contoso Way, Redmond WA
You received this because you opted in to Contoso updates.
Unsubscribe · Manage preferences · Privacy
```

## Do / Don't

- Do keep to one primary CTA per email.
- Do trace every claim to a real offer or fact from the audience file.
- Don't email rows with missing/blocked addresses — list them for the user.
- Don't send without explicit approval.
