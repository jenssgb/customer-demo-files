PROJECT BRIEF — Customer Portal 2.0
Prepared by: James Okafor, Product Manager, Nexora Inc.
Date: Thursday, May 15, 2026 (dumped in my lap at 4:47pm)

Background:
Customer Portal 1.0 launched in 2019. It's running on a monolith that nobody wants to touch.
Support tickets mentioning the portal: 43% of total volume. NPS for portal-related interactions: -12.
Customer success has been escalating for 6 months. Enterprise churn in Q1: 3 accounts cited portal friction.

What we're building:
A redesigned B2B Customer Portal that lets customers manage their licenses, submit support tickets,
view usage analytics, download invoices, and manage user access — all in one place.
API-first. Mobile-responsive. SSO via Azure AD.

Budget: $380,000
Timeline: June 2026 – December 2026 (6 months)
Target launch: January 5, 2027

Objectives:
1. Reduce portal-related support tickets by 40% within 90 days of launch
2. Increase self-service resolution rate from 18% to 55%
3. Achieve portal NPS of +30 or better by Q1 2027
4. Migrate all 847 active enterprise accounts before Feb 28, 2027

Key phases:
Phase 1 (June–July): Discovery, architecture, API design
Phase 2 (August–October): Core build — auth, license mgmt, ticket portal, analytics dashboard
Phase 3 (November–December): UAT, migration, launch prep

Known risks:
- Legacy API from 2017 has zero documentation. Backend team estimates 3 weeks just to map endpoints.
- dependency on Infrastructure team for SSO rollout — they're already at 90% capacity in Q3
- Only 2 enterprise design tokens available (Design team constraint)
- Migration window is fixed: Dec 22–Jan 4 (holiday freeze exception approved)
