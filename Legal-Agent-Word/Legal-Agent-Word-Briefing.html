Session at a glance

Contract review inside Word

A legal workflow demo for Microsoft 365 Copilot in Word: summarize a vendor agreement, inspect risk with citations, compare against a legal playbook, draft redlines, and fall back gracefully when the Legal Agent Frontier preview is not available.

Primary story

General Counsel needs a fast review of a property services agreement before signature.

Wow moment

Legal Agent in Word can cite source language and propose tracked-change redlines.

Backup

Normal Copilot in Word or Copilot Chat can still summarize, compare files, and draft review notes.

Setup

Five minutes before the demo

Preview caveat: Legal Agent is Frontier/Preview. Use it live only when Word desktop, Frontier enrollment, license, region, Office build, and tenant model settings are ready.

Legal Agent path

Open Word desktop, open the contract, open Copilot, select the plus button in the prompt box, then choose Legal Agent (Frontier).

Fallback path

Open the contract in Word and use normal Copilot, or upload all three Word files into Copilot Chat and run the backup prompts.

Contract understanding

Executive summary

Run in Legal Agent or normal Copilot in Word.

The point: Word becomes the review surface, not a place where legal work is pasted after the fact.
Summarize this property services agreement for a General Counsel. Focus on commercial structure, high-risk clauses, negotiation pressure points, and what must be escalated before signature. Keep it to 10 bullets and cite the clause numbers you rely on.

Risk scan

Ask for clause-level risk and negotiation posture.

Click citations if Legal Agent returns them. That is the trust moment.
Review the agreement for legal and commercial risk. Prioritize subcontracting, service credits, indexation, data protection, confidentiality, audit rights, liability, termination, governing law, and AI use. For each issue, explain the risk, cite the source clause, and suggest a negotiation position.

Review against playbook

Playbook comparison

Use Copilot Chat with all files if the Legal Agent cannot access the attached playbook.

The story moves from document Q&A to controlled legal process.
Compare the agreement against Contoso_Legal_Playbook_Service_Agreements.docx. Identify every clause that does not match the preferred position or fallback. Create a table with: topic, current contract position, playbook position, risk level, suggested redline, and whether legal escalation is required.

Business overlay

Bring in the counterparty memo to separate legal must-fix from business trade-off.

Use Counterparty_Position_Memo.docx as negotiation context. Which contract issues are legal must-fix items, which are business trade-offs, and which can be accepted with a comment or fallback language? Give me a recommended negotiation sequence.

Redlines and comments

Targeted redlines

Legal Agent can propose tracked changes; normal Copilot can draft clause language.

Keep the reviewer in control: propose, review, accept.
Draft negotiation-ready redlines for the highest-risk clauses. Preserve the original commercial intent where possible, but align with Contoso's playbook. Focus on: subcontractor approval, data processing purpose limitation, confidentiality survival, audit rights, liability cap carve-outs, termination fee, and AI tool controls.

Counsel comments

Use comments to create a review trail for Legal and business owners.

Insert or propose short legal review comments for the clauses that need human counsel approval. Each comment should state the issue, why it matters, and the decision needed from Legal or the business owner.
Backup

When Frontier is not available

Word Copilot

Use the open contract for summary, risk scan, and draft replacement language. Manually paste one edit with Track Changes enabled.

Copilot Chat

Upload contract, playbook, and memo together. Ask for the review matrix and signature recommendation.

All-file fallback

Works without the Legal Agent.

Use the contract, playbook, and position memo I uploaded. Act as a legal operations assistant preparing counsel for review. Do not provide final legal advice. Produce a contract review matrix with citations, risks, proposed language, and owner decisions.
