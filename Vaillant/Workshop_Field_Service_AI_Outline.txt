Workshop Outline — AI in Field Service (planning notes)

Goal: Run a 60-minute workshop with 8 field-service supervisors to gather their top pain
points and decide on 3 candidate AI pilot use cases.

Agenda (proposed):
1. Welcome + framing (5 min)
2. Where do you lose time today? (15 min — open discussion)
3. Where would faster info help most? (15 min)
4. Which 3 ideas should we pilot first? (15 min)
5. Wrap + next steps (10 min)

Participants:
- 5 supervisors from DE (Munich, Cologne, Hamburg, Berlin, Stuttgart)
- 2 supervisors from NL (Amsterdam, Eindhoven) — Dutch native, English fine
- 1 supervisor from FR (Lyon) — French native, prefers French for nuance

Note for facilitator: this is a multilingual room. Need real-time translation so French
participant can engage fully in their own language without slowing the rest of the room.

Open questions to capture:
- What customer-facing tasks would they offload first?
- What back-office tasks (paperwork) eat the most time?
- Would they trust an AI assistant for diagnostic suggestions?
- What boundary do they want around AI talking to customers directly?
