# Zava Executive Decision Memo

## Decision needed today

Zoe Keller needs a go/no-go recommendation for the Microsoft Events rush order by 17:00 today. The customer asked for 20,000 smart launch shirts delivered within 14 calendar days to Seattle, Dublin, and Singapore.

## Recommended position

Zava should accept the order with a conditional commitment:

1. Lock Berlin Printworks and Porto Apparel Lab capacity today.
2. Protect a pricefloor of EUR 25.80 per unit.
3. Disclose the White XL/XXL supply risk and propose a controlled size/color mix.
4. Confirm premium freight for Singapore before final signature.

## Executive narrative

This is a high-visibility launch order that can become a flagship reference for Zava's connected apparel platform. The operational risk is real, but manageable if the team narrows the offer, protects margin, and sequences production by risk.

## Decision criteria

- Commit only if the final customer response includes operational conditions.
- Do not discount below the finance pricefloor.
- Use Cobalt and Black as the risk absorber if White XL/XXL demand exceeds available capacity.
- Treat NFC activation capacity as the gating item, not shirt printing.

## Final recommendation

Proceed with a conditional yes. The answer should be confident, transparent, and commercially disciplined.
