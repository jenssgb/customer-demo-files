# Zava Rush Order Context

## Company

Zava is a fictional intelligent athletic-wear retailer used for Microsoft demo scenarios. The company sells connected apparel for sports, events, and corporate wellness programs.

## Situation

Microsoft has asked Zava whether it can fulfill a rush order of 20,000 smart launch shirts for a major product event. The shirts need printed event branding, NFC tag activation, and shipment to three regional event hubs.

## Customer Request

- Quantity: 20,000 shirts
- Delivery deadline: 14 calendar days
- Target answer window: today by 17:00
- Customer priority: reliable fulfillment over lowest price
- Required sizes: S to XXL
- Required colors: black, cobalt, white
- Shipment hubs: Seattle, Dublin, Singapore

## Internal Roles

- Zoe Keller, COO: final go/no-go recommendation
- Maya Chen, Sales Operations: customer response and pricing
- Omar Haddad, Supply Chain: inventory and supplier capacity
- Lena Vogt, Finance: margin and expedite cost review
- Rafael Ortiz, Retail Operations: store staffing and packing plan

## Known Constraints

- Current finished inventory is below the requested 20,000 units.
- Berlin print partner can add 7,500 units within 10 days with an expedite fee.
- Porto print partner can add 5,000 units within 12 days with lower cost but higher freight risk.
- NFC tag activation capacity is the likely bottleneck.
- Customer expects one clear recommendation, not a list of options.

## Decision Criteria

1. Can Zava commit to the full 20,000 units within 14 days?
2. What mix of inventory and expedited production reduces delivery risk?
3. What price protects at least 32 percent gross margin?
4. What risks need to be disclosed in the customer response?
5. What internal actions need owners by end of day?
